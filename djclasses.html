FAQS / DJ CLASSES DUBAI

WHERE ARE CLASSES HELD?

In-person sessions take place in a private setting in Dubai, with all the gear you need for the course — turntables, DJ controller and monitoring — in a relaxed 1-on-1 environment. Classes available from September 28.

DO I NEED MY OWN VINYL?

We provide a curated record collection for training, but we highly encourage you to start your own crate. Crate digging is a core part of the mastery.

WHAT EQUIPMENT IS USED?

Vinyl classes run on professional club-grade turntables with analog mixers. Digital classes run on a professional DJ controller with full club-standard workflow.

HOW MUCH DO CLASSES COST?

Digital DJ: 2750 AED (20 hours). Vinyl Mastery: 3750 AED (20 hours). Pro Pack (both formats): 6000 AED (40 hours) — saving 500 AED, with free professional DJ headphones delivered at your first class once payment is complete. All private, 1-on-1, in Dubai.

VINYL OR DIGITAL — WHICH ONE?

Vinyl trains your ears through manual beatmatching — the strongest foundation. Digital is today's club standard and gets you gig-ready fast. The Pro Pack gives you both: the craft and the industry workflow.

WHAT IS THE LEVEL REQUIRED?

We offer courses for all levels: Beginner (zero experience), Intermediate (digital to vinyl transition), and Advanced (performance polish).
