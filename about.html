BIOGRAPHY

THE SONIC
ARCHITECT

Javi Beat is a Dubai-based international DJ specialising in lounges, hotels, restaurants, beach clubs and rooftops. His sets blend soulful, afro and deep house, nu-disco and timeless classics, with a sound tailored to each venue and its atmosphere.

Active since 1999, Javi brings a thoughtful approach to music selection, shaping the mood from relaxed daytime sessions and sunset drinks to lively evenings. His background in vinyl, ’80s music and hip-hop adds depth to a versatile style that always puts the setting and the audience first.

He has performed at venues including Nikki Beach Mallorca, Drift Beach Club, AURA Skypool, Attiko, Lola Taberna, Varadero Mallorca and Provocateur Dubai. He is also available for private celebrations, weddings and corporate events.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What music genres does Javi Beat specialize in?

My sets blend soulful, afro and deep house, nu-disco, downtempo and timeless classics. I shape the music around each venue and its guests, from relaxed daytime sessions and sunset drinks to lively evenings, rather than playing a fixed playlist.

Do you travel internationally for events and weddings?

Yes. I am based in Dubai and available for private celebrations, weddings and corporate events in the UAE and abroad, with each set tailored to the occasion.

What is your musical background?

I have been DJing since 1999. My background in vinyl, '80s music and hip-hop shaped the way I select music. Today I play lounges, hotels, restaurants, beach clubs and rooftops, at venues including Nikki Beach Mallorca, Drift Beach Club, AURA Skypool and Attiko.
